PuTTy INSTALLATION:
===================
1.www.puTTY.org 
2.download puTTY and configure
3.open linux terminal and runcommand --> ip a or ip addr or ipconfig
4.open VM machine setting click network ==> NAT to Host only Adapter and save.
5.open puTTY 
6.enter IP from the linux and save
7.next click open and login your linux username and password.
